Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Overview

Listen This is an audiobook player app that respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Data Storage

All your data stays on your devices:

  • Audiobook files - Stored locally on your device and optionally in your personal iCloud Drive
  • Library metadata - Book titles, authors, chapters, and playback progress sync via your personal iCloud account
  • App settings - Stored locally on your device

iCloud Sync

If you use iCloud sync, your library and playback progress are stored in your private iCloud container. This data is:

  • Encrypted by Apple
  • Accessible only to you
  • Not visible to us or any third party

You can disable iCloud sync in your device's Settings app.

Audiobookshelf Connection

If you connect to a self-hosted Audiobookshelf server:

  • Server credentials are stored securely in your device's Keychain
  • Communication happens directly between your device and your server
  • We do not have access to your server or its contents

Data Collection

We do not collect:

  • Personal information
  • Usage analytics
  • Crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers

Third-Party Services

Listen This does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Listen This does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on our GitHub repository.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
